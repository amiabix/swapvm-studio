Contracts, receipts & technical evidence

What you just watched

Three positions each start with a virtual allocation of 1,000 rUTH. One wallet holds only 1,000. A 1,500 test-USD trade consumes 600 rUTH through the first position. The stock sibling still quotes 600; its transfer fails. Solvent’s asymmetric clamp quotes 240 against the remaining 400, then settles.

Revoking approval leaves 160 rUTH in the wallet but makes it unavailable to Aqua. Solvent’s full quote reverts at zero reserves; it does not return a successful zero quote.

Measured, with limits

Native asymmetric execution saved 3,096 gas against extruction in the recorded cold-swap benchmark. This sequence uses that native asymmetric mode. Proportional mode is also implemented, but its constrained-inventory additivity counterexample is retained in the test suite.

Existing mainnet-fork replay and both instruction paths remain in the repository. The agent and sponsor integrations are separate from this guided demo.
